        Nepalese spirit

        Where tradition meets the mountains

        Nepal is a land of warm hospitality, colorful festivals, ancient temples, and spiritual peace. Every valley tells a story of heritage and harmony.

        • Festivals such as Dashain and Tihar bring communities together.
        • Mountain communities preserve time-honored customs and lifestyles.
        • Ancient temples and monasteries add depth to every journey.

        Seven provinces

        Explore Nepal's diverse regions

        1. Koshi Province

        • Famous places: Mount Everest, Sagarmatha National Park, Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve
        • Resources: Hydropower, agriculture, forests, medicinal herbs
        • Animals: Red panda, Himalayan tahr, wild boar, migratory birds
        • Culture: Rai, Limbu, Sherpa, and Tamang traditions, vibrant festivals and mountain heritage

        2. Madhesh Province

        • Famous places: Janakpur, Ram Janaki Temple, Chhath Ghat, Kushinagar connection
        • Resources: Fertile plains, rice, wheat, livestock, and trade routes
        • Animals: Gharial, crocodiles, buffalo, deer, and birds of the Terai
        • Culture: Mithila art, Maithili language, folk songs, and deep Hindu traditions

        3. Bagmati Province

        • Famous places: Kathmandu Valley, Pashupatinath, Bouddhanath, Bhaktapur Durbar Square
        • Resources: Tourism, heritage industries, hydropower, services, and handicrafts
        • Animals: Langur monkeys, Himalayan birds, leopards in nearby hills
        • Culture: Newari civilization, temple architecture, festivals like Indra Jatra and Dashain

        4. Gandaki Province

        • Famous places: Pokhara, Annapurna Circuit, Manang, Muktinath
        • Resources: Tourism, hydropower, mountain agriculture, medicinal plants
        • Animals: Snow leopard, musk deer, Himalayan monal, red panda
        • Culture: Gurung, Magar, and Tibetan-influenced traditions, prayer flags, and mountain festivals

        5. Lumbini Province

        • Famous places: Lumbini, Devdaha, Tilaurakot, Buddhist pilgrimage sites
        • Resources: Agriculture, timber, rice and wheat production, natural forests
        • Animals: One-horned rhinoceros, Bengal tiger, deer, crocodiles, and birds
        • Culture: Buddhist heritage, diverse ethnic communities, and spiritual peace

        6. Karnali Province

        • Famous places: Rara Lake, Shey Phoksundo National Park, Jumla, Dolpa
        • Resources: Minerals, medicinal plants, forests, alpine pasture, hydropower potential
        • Animals: Blue sheep, snow leopard, Himalayan musk deer, wild yak
        • Culture: Indigenous traditions of Karnali people, folk dances, and remote mountain lifestyles

        7. Sudurpashchim Province

        • Famous places: Api Himal, Khaptad National Park, Dipayal, Shuklaphanta National Park
        • Resources: Medicinal herbs, forests, livestock, minerals, and mountain agriculture
        • Animals: Bengal tiger, swamp deer, peacocks, and Himalayan wildlife
        • Culture: Tharu, Magar, and hill communities; local fairs, dances, and oral traditions
